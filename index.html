FY2025 Budget Variance Review

Built an end-to-end Excel budget variance analysis across 6 departments, 10 expense categories, and 1,834 source transactions — from raw messy data to a clean summary dashboard tracking actual vs. planned spend for the full fiscal year.

  • Total actual spend came in $253K under budget (4.9% favorable variance) — Operations was the largest single contributor to savings.
  • Monthly trend analysis revealed spend volatility in Nov–Dec, suggesting a forecasting gap in end-of-year planning.
  • Built with structured formulas (SUMIF, IFERROR, INDEX-MATCH) — no macros, fully transparent and easy to hand off.

Forecasting Misoprostol Distribution Using Temporal Convolutional Networks

Built a time-series forecasting model on four years of BPOM pharmaceutical distribution data (2021–2024) to help regulators anticipate volumes and catch unusual spikes earlier. Deployed as a local Streamlit app with per-distributor forecasting.

  • Model error stayed below 6% (NMAE 0.057) — accurate enough to support risk-based monitoring decisions.
  • Identified consistent weekly seasonality and a gradual upward distribution trend across the dataset.
  • Streamlit app allows regulators to select any distributor and generate a 7–90 day forecast on demand.
